Background

All NPS units in the Cumberland Piedmont Inventory and Monitoring Network (CUPN) take part in a monitoring protocol to track the presence and extent of ozone foliar injury on target plant species. Resource Briefs that summarize findings at each NPS unit in each monitoring year can be found on NPS DataStore under project reference code 2312827. Here, we show changes in the presence and rate of both suspected and confirmed ozone foliar injury at monitored sites across the monitoring period.

When data were collected, data collectors recorded individuals with what they determined in the field to be ozone damage. We subsequently refer to these observations as “suspected ozone foliar injury.” Field crews then collected a voucher specimen consisting of three leaves from three individuals for each species in a plot that had foliar injury. These voucher specimens were then sent to an expert for verification of the cause of foliar injury. That status (positive or negative for foliar injury) is then assigned to all individuals of that species in that plot that the field crew suspected had foliar injury). We refer to foliar injury data after confirmation by an expert as “confirmed ozone foliar injury.”

Ozone Exposure Index for Vegetation

The Ozone exposure index for vegetation is the “3-month maximum 12-hour W126 index that occurs during the growing season (March–September), measured in parts per million-hours (ppm-hrs). The W126 index is a biologically relevant measure that preferentially weights the higher ozone concentrations more likely to cause plant damage.” This definition and the ozone index values come from the NPS Air Quality in Parks website. There are yearly data collected on site for some park units. For others, there is an interpolated average index for each year, which interpolates values from nearby stations and averages them over the previous five years (inclusive of the “current” year). For parks with on-site data collection, we also calculate a five year average index to allow us to compare values across all parks in the sampling protocol.

We show the presence of foliar injury (i.e. number of individuals with and without injury) and the rate of foliar injury (i.e. proportion of total observed individuals with and without injury). We also show the presence and rate of ozone injury by severity. Each of these metrics is shown grouped across all parks and all species, groups across all parks by species, grouped across all species in a park, and by species in a park.
